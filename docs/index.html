Engineered for the ESP32

Cutting-edge retro emulation optimized specifically for resource-constrained hardware.

Full GBC + DMG Support

Powered by the Walnut-CGB core. Runs both original monochrome Game Boy titles and full Game Boy Color games with dual-speed 8MHz CPU, 32KB WRAM, and authentic hardware color palettes.

10-bit PWM Audio & Menu BGM

High-resolution 10-bit PWM audio (78.1 kHz carrier) on GPIO 26 via onboard SC8002B amplifier. Real-time 4-channel Game Boy APU synthesis, SD menu music streaming (/bgm.wav), and instant runtime toggle (OFF / LOW / MED / HIGH) with full save state compatibility.

Direct SD Sector Streaming

Custom 512-byte hardware-aligned cache with pre-resolved FAT32 cluster sector lookups. Reads raw SPI blocks on-demand, achieving a locked 50+ FPS with 0 cache misses and zero PSRAM required.

Wireless Controllers

Seamless Bluetooth pairing via Bluepad32. Works with Xbox Wireless, PlayStation DualShock 4 / DualSense 5, Nintendo Switch Pro, and 8BitDo controllers with ultra-low latency radio tuning.

True Save States

Complete CPU, VRAM, and timer snapshot serialization directly to microSD. Save and resume anywhere instantly, alongside authentic battery-backed cartridge SRAM saves.

10:9 Crisp Aspect Ratio

Pixel-accurate integer scaling tailored to the 2.8" 320x240 TFT LCD with custom retro borders and vibrant backlighting.

Browser-Based Tooling

No Arduino IDE or PlatformIO installation required to get started. Flash official releases and upload ROMs wirelessly or via USB directly from your browser.

MicroSD Card & Hardware Setup

Get your CYDboy running in three simple steps.

1

Flash the Firmware

Connect your Cheap Yellow Display via USB-C to Google Chrome and click Connect & Flash CYDboy in the flasher above.

2

Format MicroSD (FAT32)

Format a microSD card (up to 32GB) as FAT32 with standard 32KB clusters. Create the following folder structure:

/roms/gb/  Classic (.gb)
/roms/gbc/  Color (.gbc)
/saves/  Saves & States
/bgm.wav  Optional menu music

3

Pair Controller & Play!

Put your Bluetooth gamepad (Xbox, PS4/5, Switch Pro, 8BitDo) into pairing mode. CYDboy connects automatically at boot!
